For visitors

Request airport pickup, hotel transfers, city rides or a private driver for a customized itinerary.

Arrange point-to-point transportation in Beirut and longer private journeys between Lebanese cities and towns.

FAQ

Common booking questions.

How do I book a ride?

Send your pickup point, destination, date, preferred time and number of passengers through WhatsApp or call +961 71 715 783.

Can I request an airport transfer?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop-off requests can be made for Beirut–Rafic Hariri International Airport and destinations across Lebanon.

Can I hire a private driver?

Yes. You can request several hours, a full day or a customized itinerary and confirm the details before booking.

Where can I travel?

Requests can include Beirut, Jounieh, Byblos, Batroun, Sidon, Tyre, Faraya, Jeita, Harissa, Baalbek, Hermel, Zahle and other Lebanese destinations.
